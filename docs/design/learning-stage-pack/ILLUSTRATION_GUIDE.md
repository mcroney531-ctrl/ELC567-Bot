# Left Illustration Guide

Use the provided inspiration boards to develop the left-side vector/illustration area.

## What we like

- soft, rounded vector/process artwork
- workflow diagrams, step flows, document/input/output metaphors
- clean white-card product illustrations
- small color accents in blue, aqua, mint, amber, and purple
- diagrams that communicate process at a glance

## What the left-side illustration should do

- support the lesson theme quickly
- be recognizable in 1 second
- sit comfortably in a relatively narrow side panel
- harmonize with the lesson workspace
- remain lighter-weight than the main instructional content

## What to avoid

- overly detailed scene illustrations
- large scenic/thematic environments
- noisy or dense infographic clutter
- full-page decorative graphics
- anything that competes with the lesson workspace

## Stage-by-stage directional metaphors

### 01 Identify
- magnifying glass over connected task cards
- problem-scoping visual
- simple diagnostic/workflow card motif

### 02 Map
- branching step blocks
- sequential process diagram
- checklist or step ladder

### 03 Envision
- organizing/synthesizing blocks into a future shape
- concept blueprint or structured stack

### 04 Refine
- find hidden details / tighten specifics
- inspection, filtering, tuning, or review motif

### 05 Deploy
- blueprint / send-to-AI / activation / handoff motif
- staged workflow being put into action
